Smart Shopper Guide

"When to Buy What" Cheat Sheet for India

Maximize savings by matching your purchase category to the deepest discount window of the year.

Smartphones & Tech Oct & Jan

Best Windows: Amazon Great Indian Festival & Flipkart Big Billion Days (Oct) for flagship iPhones/Samsung, followed by Black Friday (Nov) and Republic Day Sales (Mid-Jan) for laptops, TWS, and mid-range phones.

Tip: Stack 10% HDFC/SBI/Axis card + exchange bonus.
Fashion & Footwear Dec & Jun

Best Windows: Myntra EORS (Early Dec), Flipkart EOSS (Mid Dec), and AJIO Big Bold Sale (Late Dec) deliver true 60%–90% off winter jackets, Nike/Adidas sneakers & denim.

Tip: Wishlist sizes 24h before Insider Early Access opens.
Beauty & Skincare Late Nov

Best Windows: Nykaa Pink Friday Sale (Nov 21 – Dec 7) is unbeatable for BOGO combos, luxe skincare miniatures, and international brand launches. Myntra EORS is great for everyday makeup.

Tip: Watch for free full-size brand gifts on ₹2,000+ carts.
ACs, TVs & Appliances Dec & Jan

Best Windows: Off-season winter clearance (Croma Black Friday, Year-End Sales, & Jan Republic Day) drops split AC prices by 15%–25% before summer demand spikes.

Tip: Compare No-Cost EMI processing fee vs. upfront card discount.
Budget & Home Goods 1st Sunday

Best Windows: Meesho First Sunday Maha Sale (Dec 6) and Year-End Clearance (Dec 26–31) offer lowest prices on organizers, cookware, curtains & festive ethnic wear.

Tip: Check seller ratings & real buyer photo reviews first.
Watches & Luxury Nov & Dec

Best Windows: Tata CLiQ Black Friday (Live now) gives 12.5% instant discount on Amex/Axis/Kotak cards on top of up to 75% off designer watches, eyewear, and fine jewellery.

Tip: Use Amex or Kotak cards on Tata CLiQ for extra 12.5% off.
